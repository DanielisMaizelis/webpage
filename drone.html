DIY Drone custom PCB.

A tiny drone and a remote to fly it, both on boards I designed. Still building it.

StatusMoving to the new version
SizeAbout 10 cm
Frame3D printed

Previous version
01 — The drone

Small, and all mine.

An STM32 runs it, a gyro and a barometer track its tilt and height, and four MOSFETs drive the motors.

Previous version
02 — The remote

Two sticks, two screens.

Two thumbsticks, an OLED and a built-in e-ink display. Its radio is about to be updated too.

(03) Why I'm redoing it

I want it to run Betaflight, which no longer supports the STM32F1 I picked. So the drone moves to an STM32F411 and an ExpressLRS radio.
