Project 04 PCB + Firmware · Ongoing

GTA Radio PCB + firmware.

A fun little FM radio on a custom STM32 board. Tune it with the buttons and read the station off the display.

StatusOngoing · rev 2 on the bench
BrainSTM32
Power12 V → 5 V → 3.3 V
RadioFM, tuned on the board
(01) Why

Just a fun little project: a car-style radio built from scratch. Press a button, the station changes and the frequency lights up on the display.

On the bench
01 — Two boards

One done, one in progress.

The video shows two PCBs. The first board is finished: press a button and the display steps through the FM band, 88.1, 88.7 and on.

The second board, in the holder, is the one I'm building now, with the fixes from the first revision. The FM radio is still being placed, then it gets tuned and adjusted.

(02) On the board

Small board, whole radio.

  1. 01

    12 V in

    A switch-mode supply (SMPS) steps 12 V down to 5 V efficiently.

  2. 02

    3.3 V

    A voltage regulator takes the 5 V rail down to a clean 3.3 V for the microcontroller.

  3. 03

    STM32

    Runs the firmware: reads the buttons, drives the display and controls the radio.

  4. 04

    FM radio

    An adjustable FM receiver. Being placed and tuned on the new board.

  5. 05

    Display

    A 7-segment readout that shows which FM station you're on.

  6. 06

    SD card

    An SD card slot on the board.
